**Research Work: Transformer-Based Contextual Modeling for Predicting Calories From Recipes**

<BR>**Research Approach**: This research proposes an enhanced text-based regression framework to estimate calorie values from Bangladeshi food recipes, directly addressing the limitations of the earlier stacking-based approach. A pretrained RoBERTa-based transformer encoder is fine-tuned end-to-end, paired with a token-level attention pooling mechanism that selectively emphasizes calorie-relevant words (ingredients, cooking methods, fat descriptors) rather than relying on a single fixed sentence embedding, enabling task-specific and fine-grained contextual feature learning for accurate calorie estimation.

<BR>**Result**: The model evaluation demonstrates a **substantial and consistent improvement** across multiple standard metrics under rigorous 5-fold cross-validation. With an RMSE of **27.95** kcal, the model shows a **strong ability** to produce predictions close to actual values, while the MAE of **18.01** kcal highlights its **consistent reliability** across individual data points. The R² value of **0.934** indicates **high explained variance**, emphasizing the model's **strong generalization**. The MAPE of **5.73%** suggests that, on average, predictions remain within a **tight and dependable range** of actual outcomes, making the model well-suited for **real-world dietary applications**. Overall, these metrics underscore a **new state-of-the-art performance**, decisively surpassing both traditional baselines and the previously introduced stacking ensemble, and confirm the model's **predictive accuracy** and **practical utility** for text-based nutritional prediction.
